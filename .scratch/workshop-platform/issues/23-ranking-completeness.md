# 23 — Ranking completeness

Type: grilling

Question: How are incomplete and unscored submissions handled in results?

Answer:

- A judge submits PRD, RFC, and App ratings together; partial scorecards are invalid.
- A submission is ranked after receiving at least one complete scorecard.
- Unscored submissions appear below ranked entries as **Not scored**.
- Overall score is the mean of the three dimension averages.
- Ties resolve by higher App average, then earlier submission time.
